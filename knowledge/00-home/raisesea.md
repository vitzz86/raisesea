# RaiseSEA operating context

RaiseSEA is a Southeast Asia founder–investor matchmaking and AI intelligence platform at `raisesea.com`. Its product combines deck analysis, investor matching, mock-pitch practice, founder tools and regional startup news intelligence.

## Team

- Vito owns backend setup, credentials, production approvals and final decisions.
- Dipti collaborates through Telegram. She does not need Hermes backend access.
- Four Hermes profiles provide specialist support: News Intelligence, Social Media Manager, Software Engineer and Chief of Staff.

## Current priorities

1. Keep the autonomous news pipeline reliable and balanced across Southeast Asia, China, Japan, South Korea and global signals.
2. Make durable project knowledge available to every specialist profile.
3. Add social-content and operating workflows without weakening publishing or security controls.
4. Keep coding available through Codex, then DeepSeek, then a reliable OpenRouter fallback.
5. Use inexpensive models only for low-risk clerical work.

## Working principles

- Telegram is the shared human interface.
- Supabase is application data; this vault is durable operating knowledge.
- Git is the audit trail for canonical knowledge and code.
- Automation may publish qualified news, but public social posts, production deploys and destructive actions keep explicit human gates.
- No credentials, tokens or private personal data belong in this vault.
