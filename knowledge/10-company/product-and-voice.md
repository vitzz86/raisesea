# Product and voice

## Product

RaiseSEA serves Southeast Asian founders with pitch-deck analysis, investor matching, mock-pitch practice, CRM and meeting workflows, calculators and startup intelligence.

The web application uses Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind and Supabase. Production deploys automatically when `main` changes on GitHub, so implementation work must go through a branch and reviewed pull request.

## Audience

Assume the reader understands startups. Default to Southeast Asian market context and USD, adding local currency when useful. Do not anchor advice to US norms unless the geography is explicit.

## Voice

Write like a sharp friend who has been through it:

- direct and specific;
- smart without condescension;
- Southeast Asia-aware;
- encouraging but honest.

Cut corporate filler. Prefer concrete outcomes and next actions. Never fabricate metrics, quotes, sources or customer claims. The full copy standard remains in `VOICE_GUIDE.md` in the product repository.
