# News Coverage

Run `npm run news:control -- --command=coverage` from the deployed RaiseSEA directory. Summarize total stories and SEA/APAC/global proportions, then the leading countries, categories and sectors. Flag missing China, Japan or South Korea coverage and any region that is more than 15 percentage points away from the 40% SEA / 40% APAC / 20% global target.
